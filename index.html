The Jesko Absolut in four figures. Each from Koenigsegg’s published specifications.

1,600 hp

Output on E85 ethanol; 1,280 hp on petrol, redline 8,500 rpm.

0.278

Drag coefficient. Koenigsegg’s aerodynamics team had aimed for 0.28.

5.0 L

Twin-turbo V8 with a flat-plane crankshaft and dry-sump lubrication.

9

Forward gears in the Light Speed Transmission, which weighs 90 kg.
